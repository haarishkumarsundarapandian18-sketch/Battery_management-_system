# Battery_management-_system

Developed a complete **Battery Management System (BMS) for a CubeSat application**, covering the process from battery selection to hardware implementation. The project started with analyzing power requirements, selecting suitable battery cells, and determining the required series-parallel configuration based on voltage, capacity, current, and energy requirements. A **battery model was developed in MATLAB/Simulink** to analyze charging, discharging, voltage, current, and battery behavior under different load conditions. An **SoC estimation model** was implemented and evaluated to estimate the remaining battery capacity. Based on the simulation results, the BMS architecture was designed with **cell voltage, current, and temperature monitoring, fault detection, and protection against overvoltage, undervoltage, overcurrent, and overtemperature**. The complete circuit schematic and PCB were designed using **KiCad**, including component selection, footprint assignment, placement, routing, grounding, and DRC. Manufacturing files were generated, the PCB was fabricated and assembled, and the final hardware was tested for sensing, protection, and system functionality. Simulation results were compared with practical measurements for validation.
